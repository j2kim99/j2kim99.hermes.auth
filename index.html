Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private, single-user productivity automation operated by its owner. It helps the owner manage Google Calendar schedules and reminders and, when explicitly requested, work with the owner's Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Contacts data.

How it works

The application runs on the owner's own computer and accesses Google services only after the owner grants OAuth permission. It is not offered to the public and does not sell or advertise using Google user data.
