Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Hermes Workspace is a private, single-user application used only by its owner.

Google user data accessed

Depending on the owner's explicit requests, the application may access Google Calendar events, Gmail messages and sending functions, Google Drive files, Contacts, Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the owner.

Use of data

Google user data is used only to provide owner-requested productivity functions such as creating and reading calendar events, reminders, reading or sending requested email, and creating or editing requested files and documents.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's own computer. The application does not operate a public user database. Data may be processed locally as needed to complete the owner's request.

Sharing and advertising

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers. It is disclosed only when the owner explicitly directs the application to share or send specific content, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored credentials and generated files remain under the owner's control and may be deleted by the owner. Google access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions may be sent to the developer contact address listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
